2. Computer Vision for Blood Cell Microscopy

Sep 2026 - Present, Sakarya University, under the advisor's supervision.

- Building an image processing toolkit for stained blood smear microscopy.
- Covers the chain from raw microscope images to quantitative cell measurements: physics-based colour and stain handling, cross-slide stain normalization, nucleus and cell segmentation, and texture and shape feature extraction.
- Segmentation and quantitative extraction are validated against expert clinical annotations.

Stack: Python, PyTorch, Scikit-Learn, OpenCV.
